RentWise: investor pitch deck for a Paris PropTech startup

PUBLISHED 20255 MIN READBY VICTOR LE BRETON

SUMMARY

RentWise is a rental management app for individual landlords in the Paris region. The deck sizes the Île-de-France market at €778.1 million to €1.04 billion a year, a €311.2 million serviceable market and a €31.1 million obtainable share, with revenue from a 4.2% management fee on monthly rent.

The brief

RentWise is a web and mobile application that centralizes rental management for individual landlords in the Paris region: viewings, tenant selection, invoicing and payment tracking. The deck had to make a seed-stage case to investors: a clear problem, a credible market size, a defensible model and a go-to-market plan, in 32 slides.

What the deck covers

  • Vision and opportunity. Individual landlords face disengaged agencies on small units, heavy admin and late payments. RentWise answers with automated rent reminders, tenant pre-screening and visit management through partner Flatsy.
  • Rental market in Île-de-France. About 62% of Parisians rent, around 1.3 million people, at an average of €1,203 a month for 45 m² (INSEE).
  • SWOT and competition. Positioning against fully digital managers Flatlooker, Liberkeys and Blue, with automation plus human support as the edge.
  • Business model. A 4.2% management fee on monthly rent, a low-code build to keep costs down, and an advanced tier at €20 a month for the financial dashboard.
  • Marketing and sales. Two months of waived fees at launch, SEO on intent keywords, agency referrals and real estate influencers.
  • Financial projections. Statements, assumptions and breakeven.

Market sizing

  • TAM: €778.1 million to €1.04 billion a year, based on about 800,000 landlords in Île-de-France.
  • SAM: €311.2 million a year, assuming 30% of landlords adopt PropTech tools.
  • SOM: €31.1 million a year, targeting 10% of the SAM in the first years.

Expansion beyond Paris targets Lyon, Bordeaux and Marseille once the model is proven.
