Why Rankfolio exists

For the past four years I have modelled investment funds, structured acquisitions and financings, and presented to investor committees on portfolios of up to €500 million. That is the standard I hold content to: numbers that reconcile, sources you can check, and claims a professional reader would sign off on.

Most finance content is written by generalists who have never built a DCF or a free cash flow bridge. It ranks for a while, then gets ignored by readers who know the subject, and increasingly by AI engines that look for sources they can trust. Rankfolio exists to fix that: content that is correct first, and structured so Google, ChatGPT, Perplexity and AI Overviews can extract it and cite it.

Across 100+ projects on Fiverr, I have written company deep dives, investment explainers, real estate guides and B2B white papers for finance blogs, newsletters, fintech and proptech clients in the UK, Germany, France and beyond, in English and French. Every sample on this site is real, delivered work.
